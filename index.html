Personal Drive Sync

Google OAuth app name: Personal Drive Sync

Personal Drive Sync is a private, single-user setup. The owner of this GitHub account uses the open-source tool rclone with their own Google account to upload, download, list and verify their own working files in their own Google Drive, and to move those files between their own computer and their own Google Colab sessions.

It is not a public service: it has no other users, no sign-up, no server and no advertising.
